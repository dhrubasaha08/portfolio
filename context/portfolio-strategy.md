Give hiring managers and collaborators enough concrete information to understand how he approaches unclear operational problems and turns them into dependable software.

## Visual direction

Preserve and refine the memorable authored qualities of the original portfolio:

- A continuous deep-space journey built from the original layered stars, planets, sun, mountains, and crater.
- One high-detail astronaut as the only three-dimensional object.
- Oversized identity, asymmetric full-width editorial compositions, real project evidence, generous spacing, and simple rules.
- The original deep navy, blue, turquoise, amber, orange, and warm-white color family.
- Self-hosted Montserrat with no remote font requests.
- Native scroll-driven decorative depth without pinned sections, captured touch input, or a nested page-scroll container.

Avoid product dashboards, repetitive interface panels, numbered controls, telemetry, neural-node imagery, fabricated machinery, symbolic dioramas, gradient text, and generated-looking decoration.
